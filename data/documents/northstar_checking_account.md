# Northstar Financial Checking Account Guide

## Overdrafts

Northstar may decline transactions that exceed your available balance. If we pay an overdraft, the overdraft fee is $25 per item, with no more than three fees per business day.

## ATM Fees

Northstar does not charge for withdrawals at Northstar ATMs. For non-Northstar ATMs, we charge $2.50 per withdrawal; the ATM owner may also charge a separate fee.

## Direct Deposit

Eligible direct deposits are generally available on the business day we receive them. Payroll deposits received before 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time may be available the same day.

## Transfer Limits

External transfers are limited to $5,000 per day and $20,000 per calendar month. Transfers may be delayed for security review.
