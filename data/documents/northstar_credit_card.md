# Northstar Financial Credit Card Agreement

## APR

Purchases have a variable annual percentage rate of 19.99%. The rate may change with the prime rate after advance notice where required by law.

## Minimum Payment

Your minimum payment is the greater of $35 or 2% of the statement balance, plus any past-due amount. Payment is due by 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on the due date.

## Late Payments

If we do not receive the minimum payment by the due date, we may charge a late payment fee of up to $30. A late payment can also cause a penalty APR of 29.99% to apply to future purchases after notice.

## Foreign Transaction Fees

Northstar charges a foreign transaction fee equal to 3% of each transaction converted to U.S. dollars.

## Cash Advances

Cash advances have a 29.99% variable APR and accrue interest from the transaction date. Each cash advance also has a fee of $10 or 5% of the amount, whichever is greater.
