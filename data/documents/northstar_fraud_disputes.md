# Northstar Financial Fraud and Dispute Policy

## Card Fraud

Contact Northstar immediately if your card is lost or you identify an unauthorized card charge. We may block the card and issue a replacement with a new card number.

## Unauthorized Transactions

Report an unauthorized electronic transaction within 60 days after the statement showing the transaction is made available. Earlier reporting helps us limit additional loss and begin an investigation.

## Dispute Timelines

We acknowledge a billing dispute within 30 days of receiving it. Most investigations are completed within two billing cycles, and we provide written results when the investigation is complete.

## Card Replacement

After verifying your identity, Northstar sends a replacement card by standard mail within five to seven business days. Expedited delivery may be available for an additional fee.
