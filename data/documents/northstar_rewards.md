# Northstar Financial Rewards Program

## Points Earning

Earn 3 points per dollar on dining and travel purchases, and 1 point per dollar on other eligible purchases. Points are calculated from the purchase amount excluding taxes and fees.

## Redemption

Redeem points for statement credits, travel, gift cards, or eligible purchases in the Northstar rewards portal. Redemption values may vary by option.

## Expiration

Points do not expire while your account remains open and in good standing. Points are forfeited if the account is closed for fraud, misuse, or unpaid balance.

## Excluded Purchases

Cash advances, balance transfers, fees, interest charges, and returned purchases do not earn points.
